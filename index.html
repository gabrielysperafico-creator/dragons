The Book of Dragons

The Draconic History of Westeros

A collection of dragons from the of A Song Of Ice And Fire, featuring their names, origins, riders, and histories.

Balerion

Balerion The Black Dread was one of the largest and most fearsome dragons ever known in Westeros. Born in the days of Old Valyria, he came to Westeros with House Targaryen and became the mount of Aegon The Conqueror. During the conquest Balerion's immense size and devasting flames helped Aegon bring much of the Seven Kingdoms under Targaryen rule. He later carried several members of House Targaryen before dying in 94 AC during the reign of king Jaehaerys Targaryen.

Known Riders

  • Aegon I Targaryen
  • Maegor Targaryen
  • Aerea Targaryen
  • Viserys I Targaryen
Meraxes

Meraxes was one of the three great dragons ridden by the Targaryens during the years of the conquest. She was ridden by Queen Rhaenys Targaryen and was known for her enormous size, pale scales and golden eyes. Alongside Balerion and Vaghar, Meraxes played an important role in the conquest, helping the Targaryens defeat their enemies with dragon fire. She survived the conquest for several years before being killed during the First Dornish War, when a scorpion bolt struck her in the eye. Her death was a major loss for House Targaryen and become one of the most remembered events of the early Targaryen reign.

Known Riders
  • Rhaenys Targaryen (Conqueror)
Vaghar

Vaghar was one of the oldest and most powerful dragons in the history of House Targaryen. She was ridden by Visenya Targaryen during Aegon the Conqueror's conquest of Westeros, fighting alongside Balerion and Meraxes. After Visenya's death, Vaghar had several other riders, including prince Baelon Targaryen and lady Laena Velaryon. By the time of the Dance of the Dragons, Vaghar had become enormous and was ridden by prince Aemond Targaryen. She played a major role in the civil war before eventually dying in battle above the Gods Eye, where she fought against Caraxes.

Known Riders
  • Visenya Targaryen
  • Baelon Targaryen
  • Laena Velaryon
  • Aemond Targaryen
Quicksilver

Quicksilver was a young silver-colored dragon ridden by King Aenys Targaryen, the only son of King Aegon Targaryen and Queen Rhaenys Targaryen. During Aenys's reign Quicksilver remained his mount until his death and was later ridden by Aenys's oldest son, Aegon Targaryen, The Uncrowned. During Aegon's attempt to challenge Maegor I Targaryen for the throne, Quicksilver faced Maegor's enormous dragon, Balerion. Despite his courage, Quicksilver was no match for the older dragon and was killed during the battle on the hill of Stone Hedge along with his rider.

Known Riders
  • Aenys Targaryen
  • Aegon Targaryen (The Uncrowned)
Dreamfyre

Dreamfyre was a pale blue dragon with silver markings and one of the oldest dragons to live during the Targaryen dynasty. She was first ridden by Rhaena Targaryen, oldest daughter of King Aenys Targaryen, they shared a deep bond. Years later, Dreamfyre was claimed by Helaena Targaryen, sister and wife of king Aegon II. During the dance of the Dragons, Dreamfyre remained in the dragonpit where she would later die during the Storming, where the struture collapsed around her, crushing and killing the dragon.

Known riders
  • Rhaena Targaryen (The Black Bride)
  • Helaena Targaryen
Vermithor

Vermithor was known as the Bronze Fury, the largest dragon behind Vaghar. He was claimed by Jaehaerys Targaryen and later by Hugh Hammer during the dance. Vermithor fought for the Blacks and later died at Tumbleton, fighting several dragons before succumbing to his wounds.

Known Riders
  • Jaehaerys Targaryen
  • Hugh Hammer
Silverwing

Silverwing was a beautiful silver dragon, known for her gentle nature. Her first rider was Queen Alysanne Targaryen, the two shared a deep bond and traveled together through Westeros. During the Dance of the Dragons she was claimed by Ulf the White and survived the dance, living wild until her death.

Known Riders
  • Alysanne Targaryen
  • Ulf the White
Cannibal

Cannibal was a wild black dragon with green eyes, earning his names by feeding on ypounger dragons. He was one of the two dragons to be born wild and dying wild, never being claimed. His origin remains unknown.

Sheepstealer

Sheepstealer was a wild dragon that lived on Dragonstone and earned his name for his habit of hunting sheeps. His scales were muddy brown and he was later claimed by Nettles, a young girl who earned his trust feeding him. After Rhaenyra demanded Nettles's death, they flew again and dissapeared.

Known Riders

Meleys

Known was the Red Queen, Meleys was a scalert dragon with bright red wings and known was the fastest. Her first rider was king Jaehaerys daughter, Alyssa, and was later claimed by her niece, princess Rhaenys Targaryen. Meleys died at her first dragon battle in the Rook's Rest against Vaghar and Sunfyre, she died with her rider.

Known Riders

Caraxes

Known was the Blood Wyrm, Caraxes was a long and slender dragon with a deep red color, He was first ridden by prince Aemon Targaryen and later claimed by Daemon Targaryen. During the Dance of the Dragons, Caraxes fought for the Blacks and died at his first dragon battle against Vaghar in the Gods Eye.

Known Riders
  • Aemon Targaryen
  • Daemon Targaryen Targaryen
Syrax

Syrax was a large yellow dragon ridden by Rhaenyra Targaryen, oldest daughter of king Viserys I. She was named after a Valyrian goddness and spent much of her time in the Dragonpit, where she later died in the Storming.

Known Riders
  • Rhaenyra Targaryen
Seasmoke

Seasomke was a pale grey dragon originally ridden by Ser Laenor Velaryon, hatched in Driftmark. After Laenor dissapeared, Seasmoke remained wihout a rider until Addam of Hull claimed him and fought for the Blacks, Seasmoke was eventually killed during the battle of Tumbleton while fighting against Tessarion and Vermithor.

Known Riders

Sunfyre

Known as Sunfyre the Golden, he was the magnificent dragon of king Aegon II Targaryen. He was famous for his brilliant golden scales aand pale wings and considered the most beautiful dragon born in House Targaryen. Despite suffering several wounds in the Dance of the Dragons, fighting against Mdeleys, Grey Ghost and Moondancer, he survived until the end of the civil war before dying in Dragonstone from his wounds.

Known Riders
  • Aegon II Targaryen
Tessarion

Tessarion, known was the Blue Queen, was a beautiful blue dragon with cobalt-colored wings and a copper-colored crest. She was ridden by prince Daeron Targaryen, the youngest son of King Viserys I. During the Dance of the Dragons, Tessarion fought for the Greens and played an important role in the battle in Tumbleton, She eventually died fighting against Vermithor and Seasmoke, suffering mortal wounds.

Known Riders
